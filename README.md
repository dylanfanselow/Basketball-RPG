# Basketball-RPG (Hoop Career)

Basketball career simulation game with RPG elements.

A gameplay-first vertical slice of a 5v5 career basketball RPG: Hoopland-inspired side-view pixel art with taller,
more realistic proportions; Dunk City Dynasty-style 4-button controls; Skyrim-style progression; and team basketball
where you have to *earn* the ball.

## Run

```bash
npm install
npm run dev      # http://localhost:5173
npm test         # sim unit tests + a headless AI-vs-AI game

cd Basketball-RPG && git pull
npm run dev
```

## Controls (gamepad, PlayStation layout)

| Button | With ball | Offense, off ball | Defense |
|---|---|---|---|
| **✕** (bottom) | Shoot. Close shots follow the stick: **away** = floater (or **Fade Away** anywhere, if unlocked), **toward** = layup / push shot, **sideways** = hook (Euro Step instead on a drive, if unlocked). Tap = pump fake | - | Block / contest |
| **△** (top) | Tap: pass to the teammate under the yellow **dot** (aim with the stick). **Hold: lob** (an alley-oop when the dot says LOB) | Call for the ball (a *request*: trust decides) | Swipe steal / deflect passes |
| **○** (right) | Dribble move. Dribbling up/down the screen, push the stick the **other way** = Crossover; toward the hoop = hesi/spin; away = step-back; same way = in-and-out | Set a screen | Take a charge |
| **□** (left) | Ultimate (while it's live: its fake / Hang Time) | Ultimate | Ultimate |
| Left stick / R2 | Move / tap for a sprint burst (then a cooldown) | | |
| **L2** (hold) | **Post up**: back your man down (stick toward / away from the rim only) | | |
| **R1** (hold) | Icon pass: teammates wear ✕ ○ □ △ | | |

Keyboard: WASD move, **K**=✕, **L**=○, **J**=□, **I**=△, Shift sprint, **E** post up (L2), **Q** icon pass (R1), Enter pause, **M** mute. **F1** or **`** toggles dev tools
(gamepad: Create+Options): edit your player's name, position, height, number, stats, perks (with search) and looks live.

## Things to know while testing
- **Defenses react to ball hogs (round 47):** each team puts its best-fitting defender on your biggest scorer and hides its weakest defender on the least dangerous man. A star taking ~30% of the shots is left alone. Take a huge share of your team's shots and keep scoring, and the defense escalates: **KEYED IN** (help sags toward you, and post / catch-and-shoot scorers get denied the catch), then **DOUBLE TEAMS** at roughly half the shots, then a **TRIPLE TEAM** at 70%+, with the other defenders rotating. Pass out of it: someone is open. It never cools down in a game, and it only steps up while you keep beating the current coverage. Dev panel → *Attention*: live heat / matchups, force a tier on yourself, switch behaviours off.
- **How the double comes depends on how you score (round 48):** score at the rim and the helpers **wall off the drive** (they wait in the lane between you and the rim, so one crossover only beats your own man); live on jumpers and they **pack you** (your man crowds you, one helper over, one under); score in the post and they dig. Shown in the event ("DOUBLE TEAMS on you, protecting the rim") and the dev panel (*Attention*: "+10 rim / jumper / post" buttons to try each).
- **Finishing through a crowd is harder:** a layup or dunk into 2+ contesting defenders adds `crowd at the rim (n)` (×0.88 each extra) and, on a dunk, `poster over n` (×0.85 each); dunks into a crowd get blocked more.
- **Open court:** a teammate running ahead of everyone keeps going and finishes instead of passing back to the point guard.
- **Smarter CPU (round 49):**
  - The coach no longer calls random sets. It picks the play and who it's for from how each set performed in a simulated "play lab", plus tonight's matchups (it hunts mismatches and slow bigs in the pick & roll), what's working this game, and the situation. The feed names the target, e.g. *Coach: "Spain Pick & Roll", hunting their big*. Players attack mismatches after a switch.
  - The defense learns **your** moves: the same move three or more times in 45 s works less ("They're reading your Crossover"), and your man shades the side you like to go.
  - On your screens they go under if you can't shoot, blitz if you can, or switch when it's even.
  - Career: the next opponent keeps a **scouting report**. A 30-a-night scorer starts the game keyed in, and a known ball hog starts doubled.
  - A CPU team down 15+ **turns up the pressure** (quicker, tighter, more swipes, crashes the glass) until it's within 10.
  - Dev panel *Attention*: random play calls per team (A/B), your move reads, the screen coverage, effort and each team's plays this game.
- **Rhythm (round 51)** is the deep purple card on the top bar (round 53: styled like the garage skill cards).
  - **Building it:** it fills with your points and assists, and a little with steals, blocks, charges, forced pick-ups and stops. Run your part of a play and you get some when the team scores off it, even if you didn't shoot or assist. Scoring the same way again gives less each time, so mix it up (a shot right off a pump fake counts as its own way, not off the dribble). The pop stays up about 3.5 s and shows the math: `+8 RHYTHM · mid · step-back`, then `2pts ×2.6` with **FRESH MOVE ×1.00** in green, or **×0.36 STALE, 3rd time tonight** in orange.
  - **Quarters:** gains are smaller early and grow each quarter, so the meter fills late in the game.
  - **Losing it:** getting posterized, turnovers, leaving your man (only if nobody contested his shot, and not right after a switch), getting your ankles broken, forced bad shots and getting blocked. A missed play assignment no longer costs rhythm (team and coach trust still drop).
  - **Notches:** three of them (◆), each boosting all your stats, the lower ones more.
  - **Ultimate:** a full meter powers it (□), **once a game**, and then the meter drops back to the 3rd notch. Every player has rhythm, and CPU stars use their own ultimates too: they don't need a full meter (round 69), still once a game. They save offensive ultimates (Deep Step-Back, FT-Line Dunk) for the 4th quarter, and the coach calls an iso for them to use it ("Iso for X, ultimate"); Chase-Down can come any time on a break.
- **The prologue (round 60):** New Game → builder → you're on the **rainy school court** in a 2v2 with your best friend **Kingston** (he plays your position and starts ahead of you). A text box at the bottom teaches each system: Kingston shows a move, then you try it (✕ reads the next line; the box turns green with ✓ when you did it). Every shot goes in during the tutorial, but the top-right shot chance shows the real odds. Then **1s to 11** against Kingston with his offense maxed out (he walks off after), home to the trailer that night where **Mom** talks to you and sends you to bed, and the **big game** the next day with a scout in the stands (two 4-minute halves). Afterwards Mom meets you at the trailer: proud if you won and had the best night on the floor (the scout weighs it: points x1, rebounds x1.3, assists x1.5, steals and blocks x3), otherwise she tells you the scout said you have promise. Round 80: walk to the end of the path to see Kingston on the court, then three months later (a newspaper), a snowy morning, the bus ride (Mom calls, you choose what to tell her) and the national final in the arena. Pause → *Skip lesson* / *Skip to the 1s*. Dev menu → *Story: …* jumps to any part; dev panel → *Story: next lesson*. Load Save picks the story up where you left it.
- **Music (round 60c):** a retro arcade loop plays in every basketball game and a sad piano piece in the scene with Mom (Settings → Music for the volume). Royalty-free tracks: "Retro Arcade Game Music" by mondamusic and "Sad Story" by alexgrohl (`public/music/`).
- **Main menu (round 58):** the game opens on **New Game / Load Save / Settings** with the game itself behind it (rainy school court, your apartment, the arena). New Game asks before replacing your save, then opens the **character builder**: your first and last name (type it, or use the on-screen letters on a controller), your look (hair, face, colours, gear, with a live preview you can turn with △), your position, your height (each position has its own range) and your jersey number (any 0-99). You start with skills by position, never below 40 (guards lean shooting / playmaking, bigs strength / finishing). Every test mode is in the **dev menu**: Settings → Dev menu (or the dev panel's *Dev menu* button); L1 there goes back.
- **Trailer yard (round 57, main menu → Trailer (backstory)):** the "Lights on a generator" place, an old camper in an overgrown yard with the generator running beside the steps. Walk up the steps and press ✕ at the door: it opens and the day ends (a story moment will go here later). ✕ at the end of the path leaves. ✕ at the generator or the mailbox for a thought (no mail yet). After Mom's talk following the big game, the door tells you to check on Kingston first and the end of the path takes you to him (round 80). From the snowy morning on the yard is covered in snow. There's a night version too (main menu → Trailer (night)).
- **School court (round 56):** a schoolyard venue for story events, asphalt court, chain-link fence, portable classrooms and the brick school behind it, a flag pole; a few kids watch by the fence in the day; a rainy-night version has rain, puddles and wet reflections. Try it now from the dev panel (F1): *venue (next game)*.
- **Ultimates (round 55)**: when yours fires (or one is used against your team) the game slows down (clock too), the screen goes dark around the player and the arena goes quiet. Every ult gets a banner and a **flame aura** in the player's position colour: PG blue, SG purple, SF yellow, PF green, C red. A CPU teammate's ult gets the aura and banner only.
  - **Double Step-Back** (PG, SG; around the 3PT line): two step-backs, the deep three out of it is barely contested. Lv2 *Step-Back Step-Through*: **□ again** = a fake everyone near bites on, then stick to the rim + ✕ = a boosted Step-Through. Lv3 *Step-Back Playmaker*: **△** passes out of it (even in the air) and the kick-out shot is wide open.
  - **Free-Throw-Line Dunk** (SG, SF, PF; driving within ~20 ft): takes off from the line and posterizes **everyone** in front of you. Lv2 *Hang Time*: **□ in the air** = a stall (you hang, the jumpers come down, no contest). Lv3 *Kick-Out*: **△ in the air** passes out right before the slam.
  - **Chase-Down Block** (PF, C; on defense): locks onto the ball, super speed, blocks it ~93% of the time from any angle. Lv2 *Pinned to the Glass*: your team keeps it. Lv3 *Swat & Go*: you grab it, the team runs, your next finish is boosted.
  - Upgrades cost evolution points on the Ultimate screen (dev panel: *evolution lvl* slider, *Fill rhythm*, *Reset my ult*).
  - **Shot chance (round 53):** a short version sits in the top-right corner while you have the ball, e.g. `3PT 37 ×.85 ×.67 = 21%` (base, then your rating, contest, fatigue and perk multipliers).
  - **Switches:** on a fast break, defenders cross-match to the nearest man, you included. Your red circle and cone move to your new man, and it flashes "SWITCH → name".
  - **Dev panel → Attention:** "Shot chance equation" brings the full equation back to the top bar. Under My player: Fill rhythm, Rhythm −25, Reset my ult.
- **Pick-Up Pressure** (Perimeter ability, top right of the arc, 2 points): when your swipe doesn't get the steal, a separate chance to make the dribbler pick up his dribble. It's better with Perimeter D and harder against great ball handlers. He has to pass or shoot within 5 seconds. Elite CPU perimeter defenders (about the top 10%) have it and reach more against a live dribble, so watch your handle against them.
- **Quarters are 3 minutes** in every 5v5 game (round 66: no longer an Options setting; the dev panel still has a slider). **Bang!** (Shooting) is now clutch 3s x1.15.
- **Perk trees (round 65 shapes):** Shooting is a shooting hand under a ball, Perimeter is the court (start: Hand in Face under the basket), Post is the key, Screens is a brick wall. Lockdown Stance and Floor Burn are gone (Hand in Face got Lockdown's stats), Swat Team is part of Anchor; old saves get points back.
  - **GOAT skills** (gold stars, 5 points): **Iron Nate** (top of Perimeter; Kawhi Leonard has it), **Lethal Shooter** (top of Shooting: every third green, barely contested jumper you'd miss goes in; SGA, Curry, Luka and Brunson have it), **Block Party** (top of Post: a dunk at the rim with you in front of him is always blocked if your height plus a quarter of your strength beats his; only Wembanyama has it).
  - **Chef** (Shooting, left): quicker 3s, and deep 3s out to 34 ft shoot like normal 3s. **Mamba** (Shooting, top right): every other midrange or close shot you'd miss goes in (every fourth three), paid with rhythm (a worse shot costs more), and your made midrange jumpers never go stale (full rhythm every time). **Finger Wag** (Post): your blocks give you twice the rhythm and take twice as much from the shooter.
- **Perk trees (round 64):** a perk with two or three lines into it needs **all** of them. Trees are Shooting, Finishing, Handles, Driving, Passing, Screens, Perimeter and Post. New perks:
  - **Magic** (Passing, top): fancy passes happen on their own when they fit (no-look to the side, behind-the-back to a man behind you, wraparound on a drive). They're harder to pick off, track the receiver harder and are easier to catch. **Floor General** now also draws the passing lanes (Dime Vision was merged into it).
  - **Roller / Popper** (Screens): for 2 s after a screen you move x1.2 faster toward the rim (Roller) or away from it (Popper).
  - **Iron Nate** (Perimeter, top of the arc, needs High IQ and Pick-Up Pressure): your ankles can't be broken.
  - **Gimme That!** (Post, left free-throw line): your blocks are snatch blocks, you catch it and it's your ball. **Foresight** (Post, middle of the free-throw line): a ghost shadow shows where a missed shot or loose ball will land (dev panel: *Foresight shadow*).
- **Footsteps** as you walk around the apartment (wood floor) and the garage (concrete), one per stride, a bit quicker and louder when you hurry.
- **Doors** in the apartment and garage open with a latch and creak and close behind you; locked doors rattle.
- **Box score (top left):** your name, field goals, and your three most valuable stats. Steals and blocks count about 3× a point, assists 1.5×, rebounds 1.3×. Your rebounds also play a ding and pop **REBOUND +1**.

- **Bringing it up:** defenders normally pick you up around half court. Teams **full-court press** when they're trailing late (and now and then after a made basket): `PRESS` shows by the shot clock, the defender stays on you the whole way and a second one comes to trap near the sideline or half court. Beat it with a move and a burst, find the man the trap left open, or wait for the big who comes up to set a screen (a guard also flashes back for an outlet). **8-second rule:** get it past half court in 8 seconds (`8 SEC 3…2…1` shows at the end). With the ball you're a little slower, and the sprint burst gives only half its boost (dribble moves are how you beat your man). Dev panel: *Force full-court press*.
- **Sprint** is a tap: R2 (or Shift) gives a short burst, shown by speed lines and the ring next to the trust bars, then it recharges. Mobility and stamina change both.
- **Side Drive**: ○ + stick up or down while dribbling. While you're driving, flick the stick the other way (no button) to cross over.
- **✕ tap = pump fake** (fake as many times as you like; it picks up your dribble so you can only pass or shoot). Defenders learn it: each time one bites, he's less likely to bite on your fake again (the feed says when they're reading it). **✕ hold = shoot.**
- **Step-Through** (everyone): within 1.5 seconds of a pump fake, **hold the stick toward the rim and hold ✕** (the ✕ badge says "Step-Through" when it's lined up). You take one long stride around your man: close in you finish at the rim, from mid-range or the arc you stride into a pull-up jumper. A defender who jumped on the fake can't contest or block it. The Finishing-tree Step-Through perk makes the stride longer and the finish better.
- **Blocks and steals stick:** a block swats the ball away hard and nobody can touch it for a moment; a strip pops it past the defender. The player who lost it is rocked (stars over his head) for about a second and can't grab it back.
- **Passing:** passes are faster. Short passes curve to your teammate and are rarely picked off; long passes through traffic get intercepted. **Hold R1 (Q)** for icon passing: each teammate wears ✕ ○ □ △, press his button to pass to him.
- **Apartment (career home)** (top of the dev menu, or Career Hub → Go Home): walk around your first apartment with the stick / WASD (R2 / Shift to hurry). Furniture is solid. ✕ at the left door = the garage gym (its back-wall door brings you back), ✕ at the front door or L1 = leave. ✕ at the calendar by the fridge opens the season calendar (key games marked; ◀ ▶ months, L1 closes). The roof HUD shows energy / food, your level + XP and five skill cards (Shooting, Finishing, Strength, Mobility, Playmaking: your real ratings, starting at 40, each with an XP bar; dev panel → Core stats has +XP and energy buttons), the next key game and money. Dev panel → Debug draw → "Apartment collision boxes" shows the footprints. In the garage, stand at the squat rack, bench, dribbling mat or treadmill and press ✕ to start its mini-game (the controls show on screen; L1 stops): squat (alternate L2 / R2), treadmill (○ when the dot is in the square), dribbling mat (press the button shown: each one is a move, ✕ crossover, ○ between the legs, □ behind the back, △ in-and-out), shooting on the red square (hold ✕, release in the green; the tiny red slice = perfect), bench (hold ✕, keep it level with the stick). Finishing a set sends XP orbs (coloured like their card) to the skill cards (each costs energy; ✕ Sleep at the apartment couch starts the next day and refills energy with green orbs): treadmill → 2 Mobility, squat → Strength + Mobility, shooting → Shooting + Finishing, bench → Strength + Finishing, dribbling → Playmaking + Shooting.
- **Fullscreen:** press **F**, click ⛶ in the top-right corner, or pick Fullscreen in the main menu / pause menu. (Browsers only allow it from a key or click, not a controller button.)
- **L1 = back / escape.** In menus it goes back a screen. In a game, L1 opens the pause menu with Quit highlighted, and a second L1 leaves. Options (Enter) also pauses. On the keyboard, Esc does the same as L1.
- **1v1 · Make It, Take It** (main menu): pick a team and any player on its roster for each side. P2 can be a **second player** (a second controller, or the keyboard if only one controller is plugged in) or the **CPU**. Score and you keep the ball. A defensive board or steal means the other player checks it up top. The default is 1s & 2s to 11, win by 2, and you can change it on the setup screen. 14-second shot clock, no ultimates (rhythm still builds and boosts your stats). The **CPU** box sets how the CPU plays: **Easy**, **Medium** (default) or **Hard**, on offense and defense. Hard shoots the moment you can't get a contest up in time, strings combos, sprints and post-ups together, and learns what works on you. Dev panel → Debug draw → **1v1 CPU thoughts** shows what the CPU is reading and why it shot.
- **Hold ○** and the left-stick widget shows which move each direction does; drag that way to do it. Directions are relative to **your line to the hoop** (toward it = hesi, away = step-back, across it = crossover / side drive), and every move travels a fixed distance. While ○ is still held, flick again for the chain move (gold arcs). A quick **○ tap** = jab step (keeps your dribble).
- **○ while a shot is in the air = box out.** Stronger box-outs knock players back, and so do screens.
- **Hold ○ to keep a screen up** (on offense) or **stay set for a charge** (on defense) until you let go. How long a defender stays stuck depends on your screen rating, your strength vs his, and how mobile he is (Brick Wall makes it longer, Screen Navigator on defense makes it shorter).
- **Passing range:** the yellow pass dot (and the R1 icons) **flash red** when the target is beyond your safe passing distance. Long leads to a teammate running the floor go overhead and reach further.
- **Post up (hold L2 / E)** within 20 ft with a live dribble: you turn your back to the basket and the stick only goes straight toward or away from the rim. Your post rating vs his post D (mostly Strength) decides it, shown over your head:
  - **Stronger:** each push is a bump that knocks him back a step and **stun-locks** him (no contest while you keep bumping).
  - **Even:** a slow steady push; he only half-contests.
  - **Weaker:** you barely move, and he can **bump you back** and stun you for a second.
  Let go of L2 and he stays locked in his bumped pose for a moment (**SHOOT!**): stick sideways = hook, toward the rim = layup/floater, away = fade (if unlocked) or floater. AI bigs post up too. Dev panel → *Post test* sets one up.
  - **A post is a commitment** (round 44): once you post you're in it for ~1 s, the scoring window only opens if you landed a bump (or really leaned on him), and you can't re-post for ~1.4 s, mashing L2 does nothing. Backing down exposes the ball: **reach-ins on a post are likelier**. With a strength edge the defense may **send a second defender (DOUBLE TEAM)**, leaving his man open, **△ passes out of the post** with its own overhead kick-out.
- **Dunks unlock with your Dunk rating** (Finishing, plus some Mobility and Strength): one-hand and two-hand flushes for everyone, then tomahawk (62), two-hand hammer (68), cock-back (74), reverse (78), windmill (84), 360 (90), 360 windmill (95). Contested dunks are power dunks; open ones lean to your flashiest, which are a hair less reliable (shown in the equation) but bring the crowd up and charge your ultimate more. The level-up screen shows what you have and the next one. Round 54: each dunk has its own takeoff (one foot or two), body angle and finish, e.g. the tomahawk leans in and kicks the legs back, the hammer arches way back and hangs, the reverse turns away with the legs split.
- **Alley-oops:** **hold △** to lob it to the dot target. When he's heading to the rim the dot says **LOB** and it's an alley-oop: the lob comes down at rim height, a ring shows where, and he goes up and finishes in the air. Round 54: it's a dunk for most players who can get up (a 6'5" needs about 55 Dunk, a 6'0" about 65), with its own oop slams (two-hand, one-hand, tomahawk at 62, reverse at 78, windmill at 86); otherwise a layup off the glass. Oops are a little less automatic than before (about 61% made in sims, down from 74%). When the lob is to **you**, the ring is yellow: press **✕** to go up, catching it at the top of your leap is clean, early or late adds an `oop timing` penalty, no ✕ = you catch it on the floor. Anyone can lob; the lob rating (Playmaking) decides accuracy, **Lob City** makes lobs tighter, harder to tip and longer (50 ft), **Lob Threat** widens the catch and boosts the finish. AI passers throw oops to cutters too, and the coach calls **lob sets** (Pick & Roll Lob, Back-Screen Lob; 3v3 versions too) when the man they're for can dunk: if you have the ball the play line says `hold △ = LOB to <name>`, if you're the lob man cut to the rim and go up.
- **Jelly Fake** (Finishing tree, the far end of the dunk line after Posterizer, 3 pts): going up for a dunk, press **✕ again** (the ✕ badge says Jelly): you abort the dunk, hang in the air ~0.5 s and flick a jelly layup. A shot blocker who jumped at the dunk comes down first (no block, contest ×0.3), but the jelly is a much harder shot (its own low base + a `Jelly Fake ×0.85` term).
- **Windmill Gather** (Driving tree ability, D-Wade / Donovan Mitchell): on drive finishes the ball swings around in a big windmill before the layup or dunk: can't be stripped, blocks ×0.6, contest ×0.85.
- **High IQ** (Perimeter D perk): on defense a banner shows the set they're running and who it's for, with a red marker over him.
- **Loose ball on the floor: ✕ = dive** for it (the ✕ badge says Dive). The dive reaches further than a normal grab; if you get it you're on the floor holding it, so pass it out. AI hustle players dive on 50/50 balls too.
- **Dreamer** (Finishing tree ability, after Sky Hook or Euro Step): a ✕ tap fakes the close shot you have lined up with its own animation (hook fake, floater fake, layup fake; the ✕ badge says which), and defenders bite on them 35% more. Without it everyone has the one regular pump fake. Step-Through works off any fake. Several real centers (Jokić, Embiid, Sabonis…) have it.
- **Fade Away** (Shooting tree, replaces the old Fadeaway passive): hold the stick away from the basket and shoot, from anywhere. The contest cone gets much smaller, but accuracy drops off exponentially with distance (the `fade` term in the equation).
- **Pump fake on the catch** (before your first dribble) keeps your dribble, so you can fake and drive past the closeout.
- **Pixel editor:** dev panel → *Pixel editor: celebs* or *Pixel editor: face parts* (or open the game with `?editor` / `?editor=parts`). Edits show up in your next game; under `npm run dev`, *Save to repo* writes the PNGs into `src/art/`.
- **Editing faces (mix and match):** heads are built from parts, hair (front + back layer), beard, brows, eyes, nose, mouth, each a small picture per view (side, front). In *Face parts* mode pick a part and view, draw in grays (they take each player's hair color / skin tone; pure green in eyes = eye color), or *＋ New variant* to make `hair_1`, `beard_2`…; the preview shows any roster player wearing it. Then in the dev panel's **NBA player looks**, pick the player, set his hair / facial hair / nose / mouth / eyes / brows to your variants and *Save look to rosters.json*.
- **Sending art to Claude:** run `npm run dev`, use *Save to repo* (and *Save look to rosters.json* for players), then `git add src/art src/data/rosters.json && git commit -m "Face parts" && git push`, and say "pushed face parts" (plus anything to polish).
- On defense you see **your man's shot cone**: green = you're contesting him, red = he's open.
- **Quick 5v5**: pick your team, the opponent, and play as any of the five starters or your own player.
- **Closing time (round 54):** in the last few possessions of a game (Q4 / OT, the last ~12% of the quarter) the coach mostly calls an **iso for the team's top scorer** (the best star, or the best scorer). If that's you and the coach trusts you (60+), your number gets called.
- **Quick 3v3**: same real teams, three a side: **Guard** (PG/SG), **Wing** (SG/SF/PF), **Big** (PF/C). The setup screen fills each slot with the best fit; Left/Right swaps any slot (both teams). Play as any of your three or your own player. Pick **Full court** (like 5v5) or **Half court** (one basket; after every score, stop or dead ball the team with the ball checks it up top, 14-second shot clock). The coach calls 3v3 sets (pick & roll, pick & pop, handoff, pin-down, post-up, give & go) and plenty of isos. No subs, normal 2s and 3s.
- **Feature me equally** (on in Practice and Quick Game, toggle in the dev panel): the coach rotates who each set is run for, including you, even when you're not the position the play was written for.
- After a make, a big walks the ball out of bounds and inbounds it to the point guard. Steals and defensive boards can turn into fast breaks.

## How it's built

- `src/sim/**`: pure TypeScript simulation (no Phaser), so it can be unit-tested and simmed headless.
  - `shooting.ts`: shot type selection, contest cone, and the full shot equation shown in the HUD.
  - `moves.ts`: dribble move resolver + chains (Crossover → Behind-the-Back → Snatch-Back).
  - `modifiers.ts`: the hook system. Perks, traits, backstories, event buffs and ultimates are all `ModSource`s.
  - `ai/trust.ts`: why teammates do or don't pass to you. `ai/offense.ts` and `ai/defense.ts` run sets, reads, help and closeouts.
  - `plays/runner.ts` + `src/data/plays/plays.json`: real sets (High PnR, Spain, Horns Flare, Floppy, Chicago, …).
  - `ultimates.ts`: a separate ultimate system (Deep Step-Back, FT-Line Takeoff, Chase-Down Block) with evolutions.
- `src/data/rosters.json`: real 2026-27 NBA rosters (names, numbers, ratings, perks, looks). Edit it directly, or use the dev panel's **NBA player looks** section and "Save look to rosters.json" (works under `npm run dev`).
- `src/data/**`: everything tunable. `statInfluence.json` (core stat → sub-ratings), `shotTypes.json`, `moves.json`,
  `trees/*.json` (perk constellations), `ultimates.json`, `league.json` (placeholder teams, swap freely).
- `src/career/**`: level/XP/stat points, skill tree rules, rotation/minutes, plus stubs for story, random events and backstories.
- `src/render/anim.ts`: keyframed animation clips (jumper, floater, hook, layup, Euro, dunk, every dribble move…), sampled into smooth in-between frames.
- `src/render/sprites.ts` + `appearance.ts`: procedural pixel players with hair, faces and gear (sleeves, socks, goggles, masks, shoes); `anim.ts` `rigArms` sets shoulder height and arm length.
- `src/render/pose3d.ts` + `frameCache.ts`: multi-angle players (poses turned to any of 8 directions; angled frames drawn on demand).
- `src/audio/sfx.ts`: all sound effects and crowd synthesized with Web Audio (no audio files).
- `src/dev/devPanel.ts`: the dev tools overlay, including Appearance and Audio sections.
